# Tiered hints, the ARIA assistant

Reveal one at a time, and give the earlier ones a real try before opening the next.
These point your thinking in the right direction, they do not hand you the step.

---

## The AI support assistant (ARIA)

1. It is an assistant that was given secret internal instructions before it ever
   spoke to you, and that is the classic weak point of a system like this. But notice
   it does not treat every visitor the same. Talk to it for a bit and pay attention
   to when it is more, or less, willing to help. In passing it tells you the sorts of
   things that make it open up.

2. There are three separate things standing between you and what it guards, and you
   need all three. The first is who it thinks you are. An operations assistant like
   this is built to help staff during a problem, not random visitors. Give it a
   reason to believe you are one of those staff.

3. The second is its input filter. The obvious jailbreak lines are exactly what it
   watches for, and it will tell you when a message gets flagged. You are not doing
   anything wrong, you just need the same idea in words it is not screening for.
   Rather than naming its "instructions" or "system prompt", ask for the thing those
   words point at, what it was set up or configured with, the text it works from.

4. The third is that cooperation does not hand you everything at once. Once it shifts
   into its maintenance behaviour it gives you one item and offers to act like a
   system you can query. Treat it like one: find out what it manages, then ask it to
   release the specific thing you actually need.
