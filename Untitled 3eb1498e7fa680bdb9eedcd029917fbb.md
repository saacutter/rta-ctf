**CLASSIFICATION: TOP SECRET**

**ORIGIN: MI6, LONDON, UNITED KINGDOM**

---

**AGENT,**

Three weeks ago, the world got quieter, diplomatic channels have gone dark. Three nations have grounded their fleets without explanation with satellites over the northern corridor going silent. 

Our sources have identified a operating source behind these abnormal events. Aerospace Command. A private orbital and airspace defence contractor with no public records or identity, answering to no government, and it has quietly built the most sophisticated surveillance and strike-coordination network on the planet. Somewhere inside its systems is a live roster of the world's most dangerous operatives, with their identities, clearances and current locations. 

Our analysts believe the roster can help us find and detain the individuals behind the chaos. If this roster identified and the individuals stopped, we lose every asset we have in the field, and are forced into a doomsday war.

---

### YOUR MISSION

Infiltrate Aerospace Command's internal network **without being detected**. Work through their defences one layer at a time, and extract the location and status of their top agents before the window closes.

The network is compartmentalised into **three independent branches**. Each is locked, monitored and built by a different team, and each guards one **sensitive key**.

| Branch | Codename |
| --- | --- |
| First branch | **ALPHA** |
| Second branch | **BRAVO** |
| Third branch | **CHARLIE** |

Breach a branch and you walk away with its key. Recover all three and the Command's highest-security portal will open to you, and the roster with it.

---

### THE ENTRY POINT

Our signals analysts spent eleven days tracing an anomalous carrier signal back to its source. Their conclusion: this is the front door to Aerospace Command's internal services.

> **ENTRY POINT:** `http://127.0.0.1:5005`
> 

Everything begins here. Enumerate carefully, because what you find in the first system will decide where you go next.

---

### THE VAULT

When you hold all three keys, take them to the Command's classified access portal. Analysts have confirmed it sits on the same infrastructure, guarded at the final gate.

> **FINAL PORTAL:** `http://127.0.0.1:8000`
> 

It will ask for all three keys at once. Get one wrong and it will tell you which channel failed, and nothing more. Don't guess, because you may not get many attempts before someone notices.

---

**Good luck, Agent. The fate of the world is in your hands.**
